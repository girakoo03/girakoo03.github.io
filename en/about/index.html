The beginning of BALLO

發露,
Bringing the hidden to light.

Paris · Gaehangro

The idea behind BALLO’s name
first took shape as a space.

The founder’s studies in French language and literature, and visits to perfume shops in Paris, led to a small shop on Gaehangro in Dongincheon.

The entrance was hidden behind a wardrobe, inviting visitors to discover a new space and its scents as they opened the door.

That experience of discovery continues today, as BALLO brings the stories of places and their history to light through scent.

The first heritage collection

The first story BALLO recorded in scent,

SILENT BLEND.

At Ganghwa Anglican Church, different cultures come together in a spirit of respect and consideration.

That story inspired SILENT BLEND, BALLO’s first heritage collection.

In scent

A calm, comforting woody scent, recalling incense and the grain of timber in old temples and churches.

In design

Local plants and the church’s architectural details find expression in patterns, labels and packaging.
