Fragrance notes

From a bright opening
to a warm, woody finish.

A soft, enveloping woody fragrance, recalling the traces of incense that linger in the timber of an old temple or church. Bright citrus gives way to soft florals. Sandalwood, vanilla and musk bring a gentle woody warmth to the finish.

Placement & care

Before you place your diffuser.

Five reeds are included.

  1. A stable surface

    Choose a flat, stable surface where the bottle can stand upright.

  2. Using the reeds

    Start with 2–3 reeds and keep the remaining reeds for later use.

  3. If the fragrance becomes faint

    Turn the reeds upside down and place them back in the bottle. If the fragrance still seems faint, replace them with fresh reeds.

Scent diffusion may vary with the space and conditions of use. Please read the instructions on the product.
