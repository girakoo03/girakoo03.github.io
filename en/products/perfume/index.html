The story behind the woody scent

A choice to plant together,
remembered in a woody scent.

In the story of a scholar tree and a bodhi tree planted side by side, we found consideration and respect. Together with the meeting of traditional Korean hanok architecture and a basilica plan, that story inspired SILENT BLEND.

Fragrance notes

From a bright opening
to a warm, woody finish.

A soft, enveloping woody fragrance, recalling the traces of incense that linger in the timber of an old temple or church. Bright citrus gives way to soft florals. Sandalwood, vanilla and musk bring a gentle woody warmth to the finish.

Product & packaging

Even the box around the fragrance
carries traces of a place.

A pattern inspired by Ganghwa mugwort sits alongside BALLO’s emblem. The angular outline of the label draws on the timber joinery of traditional Korean hanok architecture.
